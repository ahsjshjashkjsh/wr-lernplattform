## Design Context

### Users
Swiss vocational students (HMS Handelsmittelschule Zürich, class H23b) preparing for final exams in Wirtschaft & Recht (WR) and Finanz- und Rechnungswesen (FRW). Context: focused study sessions, often under time pressure before the Abschlussprüfung 2026. Job to be done: learn efficiently, practise booking entries and quiz questions, get quick answers from the AI assistant.

### Brand Personality
Professional · Precise · Trustworthy. The interface should evoke **confidence and calm focus** — like a well-designed reference tool. Students should feel they are using a serious, competent product.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Refined Swiss Utility Dark.** Dark mode as default (deep navy #060a14 bg). One primary accent colour: a precise, saturated indigo (#4f72f5) used sparingly and consistently. No gradient blobs, no glassmorphism decoration, no neon glows, no rainbow card borders. Solid surfaces, clean borders, strong typographic hierarchy. Think: the precision of Swiss graphic design applied to a study SaaS. Anti-reference: "vibecode" aesthetics — blue-purple gradient text, glowing icon boxes, glassmorphism cards, radial blob backgrounds.

### Design Principles
1. **One accent, used sparingly.** A single indigo (#4f72f5) for interactive/active states. Emerald for FRW, a softer blue for WR only as contextual semantic hints — never competing for attention.
2. **Solid surfaces over glass.** Cards use `--card-bg` (solid #0c1525), not transparent + backdrop-filter. Noise-free.
3. **Typography carries hierarchy.** Headings are large and confident, weight contrast does the work. No gradient text effects.
4. **Cohesion over colour chaos.** Card borders are all the same (`--border-color`). Category colour lives inside the content (chips, icons), not on the container.
5. **No visual decoration that doesn't serve function.** Radial gradients, glow box-shadows, and decorative blobs are removed. Whitespace is the decoration.
