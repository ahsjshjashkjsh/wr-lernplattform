# WR RULES

## LEGAL STRUCTURE
Each concept must include:
- Voraussetzungen
- Tatbestand
- Rechtsfolge
- Abgrenzung

## EXPLANATION
Must be:
- precise
- logically structured
- not superficial

## CONNECTIONS
Show relationships between:
- legal actors
- obligations
- consequences

## SUMMARY
Must fully explain the legal concept so it is understandable alone.
